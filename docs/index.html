What it does

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

GateScout pulls live data from CCP's ESI API and zKillboard and puts it exactly where you need it.

💀

Live Kill Tracking

Ships and pods destroyed in the last hour, per system. Updated every 30 seconds so you're never flying blind.

🚀

Jump Traffic

Real-time jump counts show you how busy each gate is. High traffic means more eyes on the gate.

📍

Gate Proximity

Kills are classified as near a specific stargate or deep space — spot gate camps before you land in one.

🔔

Sound Alerts

Audio beep the moment new kills appear in a neighbouring system between refreshes. Mute with one click.

🔍

Kill Summaries

Click any kill to see the full breakdown — victim, corp, ISK value, final blow attacker, and fleet composition.

🪟

Non-Intrusive Overlay

Frameless, transparent, always-on-top. Drag it anywhere, resize it, or collapse it to a single bar.

Getting started

Up and running in minutes.

No Python. No command line. Just download, install, and log in.

01

Download

Grab the installer from the Releases page. One file, no admin rights needed.

02

Install

Run the installer. Next → Install → Finish. GateScout appears in your Start Menu.

03

Login

Click Login, authenticate with your EVE account in the browser, and you're live.

04

Fly safe

Jump in-game. GateScout detects your system and shows live intel on your neighbours.

Reading the overlay

Colour-coded threat levels.

Kill counts are colour-coded so you know the threat level at a glance — no numbers needed.

Green — Clear

0 kills in the last hour

Yellow — Caution

1–2 kills detected

Orange — Warning

3–4 kills, hostile activity likely

Red — Danger

5+ kills, find another route
